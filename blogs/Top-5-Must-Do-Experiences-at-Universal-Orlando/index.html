Top 5 Must-Do Experiences at Universal Orlando Resort

By Thrill and Chill Travel | June 18, 2025

Planning a trip to Universal Orlando? With three incredible parks and a bustling entertainment district, it’s hard to know where to start. Here are the top 5 things you absolutely can’t miss at Universal Orlando Resort — curated by your travel experts at Thrill and Chill Travel.

1. Hagrid’s Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure

This isn't just a ride — it's an epic journey through the Forbidden Forest on a flying motorbike. Hagrid’s ride is packed with drops, surprises, and animatronic magic. It’s one of the most popular coasters in the world for a reason.

Pro Tip: Use Early Park Admission to skip the long lines. We can help you get it with your package — request your free quote today!

2. The Wizarding World of Harry Potter

Wander through Diagon Alley and Hogsmeade, sip Butterbeer, and ride the Hogwarts Express between parks. The detail and immersion are unmatched.

Pro Tip: To ride the Hogwarts Express, you’ll need a Park-to-Park ticket — we’ll help you find the best deal.

3. Volcano Bay Water Theme Park

Universal’s tropical-themed water park combines thrill rides with laid-back relaxation. With TapuTapu wearable tech, you can reserve your spot in line while you float down the lazy river.

Pro Tip: Book a cabana for the full VIP treatment — ask us how.

4. CityWalk Dining and Entertainment

CityWalk is a food lover’s paradise with iconic stops like Toothsome Chocolate Emporium and Voodoo Doughnut. No park ticket required!

Pro Tip: Planning a non-park day? CityWalk is the perfect place to eat, shop, and unwind. Let us help you plan the perfect itinerary.

5. Universal’s Nighttime Shows & Events

Don't miss the cinematic lagoon show, or special events like Halloween Horror Nights. Universal Orlando comes alive after dark.

Pro Tip: Check show schedules in advance and arrive early for the best viewing spots. Want VIP access? We’ll show you how.
