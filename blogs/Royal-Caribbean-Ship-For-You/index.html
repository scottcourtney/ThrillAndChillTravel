Which Royal Caribbean Ship Is Right for You? (A Guide for Every Traveler)

By Thrill and Chill Travel | July 12, 2025

With over two dozen ships in their fleet, Royal Caribbean has a cruise experience for every type of traveler. Whether you're traveling solo, bringing the whole crew, or craving non-stop thrills, there’s a ship designed with you in mind. Here’s your go-to guide to picking the perfect Royal Caribbean ship.

🌟 For First-Timers or Families: Wonder of the Seas

As the largest cruise ship in the world (for now), Wonder of the Seas is packed with family-friendly entertainment: water slides, zip lines, surf simulators, a carousel, and more. Kids will love the Adventure Ocean youth program, while adults can unwind in the Solarium or spa.

🎉 For the Young (and Young at Heart): Icon of the Seas

This ship redefines cruise fun. With seven pools, a record-breaking waterpark at sea, and next-level nightlife, Icon of the Seas is built for social travelers who want action all day and DJs all night. Perfect for birthday trips, girls’ getaways, and multi-gen travel.

🧘‍♀️ For Couples or Chill Seekers: Odyssey of the Seas

Sleek, modern, and designed for relaxation, Odyssey of the Seas features an adult-only Solarium, world-class cuisine, and laid-back destinations. The North Star viewing capsule and bionic bar make it Insta-worthy too.

🛳️ For Cruise Purists: Radiance- and Vision-Class Ships

Smaller and more intimate, ships like Brilliance of the Seas or Enchantment of the Seas offer classic cruising with plenty of ocean views and a more traditional atmosphere. Great for seasoned travelers or those who prefer port-heavy itineraries.

🚀 For Thrill Seekers: Symphony of the Seas or Harmony of the Seas

If you want a nonstop adrenaline rush, go for the ships packed with zip lines, laser tag, Ultimate Abyss slide, FlowRiders, and escape rooms. These mega-ships are floating amusement parks — and you'll never run out of things to do.
