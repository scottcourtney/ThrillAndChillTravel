Ranking Every Disney Park Ride: From Thrills to Classics

By Thrill and Chill Travel | July 12, 2025

There are more than 50 rides across the four parks at Walt Disney World — and let’s be honest, they’re not all created equal. Whether you’re chasing adrenaline or sharing nostalgic moments with your kids, here’s our ultimate ranking of Disney’s best rides, broken down by type.

🎢 Top 5 Thrill Rides

  1. Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind – EPCOT’s newest and smoothest coaster. Total re-ride value: 10/10.
  2. Tron Lightcycle / Run – A high-speed, futuristic launch coaster at Magic Kingdom that feels like flying.
  3. Expedition Everest – Animal Kingdom’s iconic coaster with a surprise Yeti and a backwards drop!
  4. Rock ‘n’ Roller Coaster – Hollywood Studios delivers speed and style with Aerosmith.
  5. Space Mountain – A retro classic that’s still a rite of passage for Disney thrill seekers.

🌟 Top 5 Classic Disney Attractions

  1. Pirates of the Caribbean – Iconic, immersive, and full of swashbuckling fun.
  2. Haunted Mansion – Spooky, silly, and a masterclass in theme park storytelling.
  3. Jungle Cruise – Classic Disney humor and charm — with a good dose of dad jokes.
  4. It’s a Small World – Love it or hate it, it’s timeless Disney magic.
  5. Peter Pan’s Flight – Short ride, long wait, but still a beloved fantasy escape for all ages.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Best Family-Friendly Attractions

  • Remy’s Ratatouille Adventure – A 4D family thrill through Gusteau’s kitchen.
  • The Seas with Nemo & Friends – A gentle ride that blends real aquariums with Pixar storytelling.
  • Frozen Ever After – Great for fans of Anna, Elsa, and beautiful animatronics.
  • Kilimanjaro Safaris – See real African wildlife up close, no two rides are the same.
  • Buzz Lightyear’s Space Ranger Spin – A family favorite that’s part ride, part game.
